# အဘိညာဉ် ၆ ပါး — Roadmap (Visuddhimagga / Pa-Auk map)

**The short answer to your question:** No — breathing in/out and noting the air alone does NOT cover all six.
Per the classical manual, ānāpāna carries you to the 4 jhanas and (with insight work) to **#6**.
Powers **#1–5** require *switching objects* after 4th jhana — from breath to **kasina** — because a breath-nimitta can't be stretched, moved, or projected the way a kasina sign can. Breath = the engine. Kasina = the steering wheel for powers.

**Legend**
- ✅ real, measurable, documented in modern practitioners
- ⚠️ real but do it with a teacher (intensive practice can destabilize people)
- 📜 tradition-only — described in texts, zero verified cases in recorded history

---

## Stage 0 — Foundation (start now, ~3 months)
Goal: a mind that can sit still. ✅

- **Sīla first.** The manual is blunt: concentration doesn't develop on a guilty conscience. Keep the 5 precepts clean.
- Daily ānāpāna: 30–60 min, same time daily. Consistency beats intensity.
- One spot only: breath sensation at nostril/upper lip. Gatekeeper — don't follow the breath in or out.
- Counting drill: in+out = 1 … up to 8, restart. Drop counting once the mind stays 10+ min.
- Milestone to exit stage: attention holds the touch point ~30 min with only brief wobbles.

## Stage 1 — Access concentration + nimitta (months → 1 year+) ⚠️
Goal: the light shows up and stays.

- Practice lengthens naturally (60–90 min sits).
- **Nimitta** appears — mental light/glow near the touch point. Common errors: chasing it, staring at it, getting excited. All three kill it. Ignore it until it's stable and merges with the breath.
- When nimitta is bright + stable for an hour = **upacāra samādhi** (access concentration).
- Reality check: most people need retreat conditions (a week to months, full-time) to cross this stage. Daily-life practice alone usually plateaus here.

## Stage 2 — The four jhanas via ānāpāna (years 1–3) ⚠️✅
Goal: absorption, then mastery. This is the part fMRI studies have actually captured.

- Mind sinks fully into the nimitta = **1st jhana** (vitakka, vicāra, pīti, sukha, ekaggatā).
- Then the **five masteries (vasī)** on each jhana before moving up:
  1. Advert to it 2. Enter at will 3. Stay a resolved length 4. Exit on time 5. Review the factors
- Progress 1st → 2nd → 3rd → **4th jhana** (equanimity + one-pointedness only, breath becomes imperceptible).
- Where: this realistically means long retreats — Pa-Auk centers run exactly this curriculum with interviews.
- Exit milestone: enter/exit any of the 4 jhanas, in order and on demand.

## Stage 3 — Kasina expansion (full-time practitioner territory) ⚠️📜
Goal: the launchpad the texts require for powers. **This is where breath alone stops being enough.**

- Take up the kasinas (earth, water, fire, air, blue, yellow, red, white...). White kasina is the classic post-ānāpāna entry.
- Develop all **8 attainments** on them: 4 rūpa jhanas + 4 arūpa states (infinite space, infinite consciousness, nothingness, neither-perception-nor-non-perception).
- Then the **14 mastery drills** (Vism XII): run jhanas up the kasinas, down, skipping jhanas, skipping kasinas, both — until the mind moves through absorptions like flipping switches.
- Note: Uddaka Rāmaputta's level (8th attainment) sits inside this stage — and the Buddha still walked away from it.

## Stage 4 — Abhiññā 1–5 (📜 tradition-only from here)
Per Vism XII–XIII, each power is produced the same way:

1. Enter 4th jhana (on a kasina base)
2. Emerge
3. **Resolve** (adhiṭṭhāna): "May I be many / hear distant sounds / know that mind / see past lives / see beings passing on"
4. Re-enter jhana, emerge — the knowledge/power manifests

Traditional order: ၁ ဣဒ္ဓိဝိဓ → ၂ ဒိဗ္ဗသောတ → ၃ စေတောပရိယဉာဏ် → ၄ ပုဗ္ဗေနိဝါသ → ၅ ဒိဗ္ဗစက္ခု.
Honest label: no human has ever demonstrated #1–5 under observation. Even the canon calls them rare among arahants. Treat this stage as the mythology layer on top of a real skill stack.

## Stage 5 — #6 အာသဝက္ခယဉာဏ် (the only one that = liberation) ✅⚠️
Goal: uproot, not suppress — the rock lifted off the grass, roots pulled.

- Method: **vipassana** — and here your Mahasi noting retreat plugs back in. This stage doesn't need Stages 3–4 at all ("dry insight" path).
- Pa-Auk version: use jhana as the base → discern mind/matter (nāmarūpa) → dependent origination (their students trace past lives here — #4-adjacent, within their framework) → the 16 insight knowledges → path & fruit.
- The map's punchline: #6 is reachable without a single power, and #1–5 without #6 leave you exactly where Ālāra Kālāma ended up — powerful and still stuck.

## Practical next steps (this year)
1. Start Stage 0 today — 30 min ānāpāna daily. That's the whole assignment.
2. At the 3-month mark, book a 10–14 day retreat (Pa-Auk-lineage if you want this exact curriculum; Mahasi-lineage if you want the #6 express lane you already know).
3. Long game: powers track = renunciant-level commitment. Insight track = compatible with a career and a mortgage.

*Sources: Visuddhimagga ch. III–XIII; Pa-Auk Sayadaw, "Knowing and Seeing"; Mahasi Sayadaw, "Manual of Insight". States in Stages 0–2 are documented in modern neuroscience; Stage 4 is scripture, not evidence.*
