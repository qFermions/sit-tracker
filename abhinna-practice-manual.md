## 1. The Governing Framework — ဣဒ္ဓိပါဒ် ၄ ပါး (SN 51.20)

The canon's answer to "how are powers developed" is not a trick — it's four qualities each fused with concentration:

1. **ဆန္ဒ (chanda)** — desire/zeal for the goal
2. **ဝီရိယ (viriya)** — energy
3. **စိတ္တ (citta)** — mind/commitment
4. **ဝီမံသ (vīmaṃsā)** — investigation

The sutta's tuning instruction, applied to every sit: energy **not too slack, not too tense; not constricted inward** (dullness), **not scattered outward** (distraction). That is the master dial for everything below.

## 2. PART A — Ānāpāna to the Fourth Jhana (the engine)

### The daily sit script (Pa-Auk method, Knowing and Seeing ch. 1)
1. Sit erect, eyes closed. 3 slow breaths to settle.
2. Place attention where breath touches — **nostril rim or upper lip**. One spot. Never follow the breath inside the body, never outside. (Vism's gatekeeper: he checks people *at the gate*, he doesn't walk with them.)
3. Know the breath as just **in / out**. Long as long, short as short. Don't control it.
4. **Counting stage:** in-out "one" … in-out "eight," restart. Count *after* each breath ends. Stay ≥30 min. Drop counting when the mind stays 15–20 min without wandering.
5. **Whole-breath stage:** know beginning-middle-end of each breath at the touch point.
6. **Subtle-breath stage:** breath becomes faint — do NOT breathe harder to find it. Keep attention on the spot and *wait*; calmness makes it reappear.

Dose: 1–2 hrs/day minimum, in as few sittings as possible. Every gap resets progress — the texts are explicit that this stage is dose-dependent.

### Nimitta protocol (this is where most people fail)
- A light/mist appears near the touch point. **Ignore it.** Stay with the breath.
- It matures: grey smoke → white cotton → bright/clear (sun-through-mist, morning star). Colors vary by perception — all fine.
- Only when nimitta and breath **merge as one** and the light is stable for ≥1 hour do you shift attention from breath to nimitta.
- Never play with it (stretch, move, push) at this stage — it shatters. Attend to it as "breath, breath."

### Jhana entry + verification (Pa-Auk standard)
- Mind sinks into the stable nimitta = **absorption (appanā)**. First sits may last minutes; extend by resolve.
- After emerging, advert to the **mind-door (bhavaṅga, at the heart base)** and check the five factors present: ဝိတက် vitakka, ဝိစာရ vicāra, ပီတိ pīti, သုခ sukha, ဧကဂ္ဂတာ ekaggatā = 1st jhana confirmed.
- **Five masteries (vasī) — drill each jhana before climbing:**
  1. Advert to factors at will 2. Enter at will 3. Stay the exact resolved time ("30 min," then hours) 4. Emerge on time 5. Review factors
- Climb by seeing the lower jhana's coarseness: drop vitakka+vicāra → **2nd**; drop pīti → **3rd**; exchange sukha for upekkhā → **4th** (breath imperceptible, mind like a mirror).
- Benchmark before Part B: sit **2–3 hours absorbed in 4th jhana at will**.

## 3. PART B — Kasina Expansion (the steering wheel)

Powers 1–5 need a *manipulable* object. Breath-nimitta can't be moved; kasina signs can. Two entry routes:

### Route 1 — Internal (Pa-Auk sequence, recommended)
1. In the light of 4th-jhana concentration, discern the **32 body parts** in yourself, then in a being near you.
2. Take the **white of the skull/bones** as object: "white, white" → it becomes a white circle = white-kasina nimitta.
3. Stabilize → expand it **one hand-width at a time** → fill all ten directions, boundless white.
4. Develop jhanas 1–4 on it. Repeat for the other colors (blue/brown from hair, yellow from fat/urine, red from blood), then earth, water, fire, wind, light, space.

### Route 2 — External (classical Vism IV, the physical disk)
- Make a disk of **dawn-colored clay**, one span + four fingers wide (~30 cm), smooth as a drumhead, on cloth/board.
- Sit **2.5 cubits (~1.1 m)** away, disk at knee height. Eyes half open — as if seeing your face in a mirror, not staring, not scanning.
- Recite mentally: **"ပထဝီ, ပထဝီ / earth, earth"** hundreds of times. Close eyes periodically — when the disk appears *equally clear with eyes shut* = **uggaha-nimitta**. Leave the disk, guard the image.
- It purifies into a polished, luminous version = **paṭibhāga-nimitta** → expand to ten directions → jhanas on it.

### The 8 attainments + arūpas
From any kasina except space: expand boundless → advert to the **space left by its removal** = ၅ ākāsānañcāyatana → the consciousness that knew it = ၆ viññāṇañcāyatana → its absence = ၇ ākiñcaññāyatana → ၈ nevasaññā-nāsaññāyatana. (This 8th is Uddaka Rāmaputta's summit.)

### The 14 masteries (Vism XII — the powers prerequisite)
Drill until effortless: jhanas up the 8 kasinas in order / reverse / both; jhana-skipping (1st→3rd…); kasina-skipping; both at once; transposing factors; transposing objects; defining each factor; defining each object. The text: the mind must run through attainments **"like Sāriputta"** — instantly, in any order. This is why it's a renunciant's career, not a hobby.

## 4. PART C — The Five Mundane Powers (Vism XII–XIII, verbatim method)

**The universal engine:** enter 4th jhana on a kasina (the *pādaka-jjhāna*, base jhana) → emerge → do the preliminary work (parikamma) → make the **resolve (adhiṭṭhāna)** → re-enter → emerge → the knowledge occurs.

1. **ဣဒ္ဓိဝိဓ (multiplication, flying, water-walking):** emerge from 4th on earth-kasina → resolve "may I be a hundred" / for flight, resolve **space or earth kasina over the path** ("may this be earth") → re-enter → act. Vism XII lists each feat with its required kasina (water-walking = earth-resolve on water; fire-body = fire kasina, etc.).
2. **ဒိဗ္ဗသောတ (divine ear):** base jhana → advert to gross distant sounds, then subtler and farther, resolving to hear across increasing range.
3. **စေတောပရိယဉာဏ် (minds):** develop divine eye first → observe the **color of the blood at another's heart-base** (bright = joy, dark = anger…) → from reading the color, incline to the mind itself and read it directly. (Yes, that's really the Vism XIII procedure.)
4. **ပုဗ္ဗေနိဝါသ (past lives):** emerge → review your last act of sitting → run memory **backwards** through the day, week, year, to birth, then incline through the death-moment into the previous life. Where memory jams, re-enter base jhana and press again.
5. **ဒိဗ္ဗစက္ခု (divine eye):** extend the **light kasina** (or white/fire) → resolve to see beings beyond walls, then dying-and-rearising according to kamma. Extend range with practice.

## 5. PART D — #6 အာသဝက္ခယဉာဏ် (the liberation one)

Does not require Part C at all. Pa-Auk sequence on the jhana base:
1. **Rūpa:** four-elements meditation → see the body as ရုပ်ကလာပ် (kalāpas) → analyze 28 kinds of materiality.
2. **Nāma:** discern mind-moments and factors in each cognitive series.
3. **ပဋိစ္စသမုပ္ပါဒ်:** trace your own mentality-materiality backwards across lives (the system's built-in taste of power #4) and forward — see causes.
4. **The 16 insight knowledges:** arise-and-pass → dissolution → fear/danger/disenchantment → equanimity → path & fruit. Repeat for higher paths until the āsavas are cut.

## 6. Schedule & Commitment Ladder

| Level | Practice | Realistically reaches |
|---|---|---|
| **Now → month 3** | 1–2 hr ānāpāna daily, counting → whole breath. Precepts clean. | Stable attention, early nimitta flickers |
| **Months 3–12** | 2 hr daily + one 10–14 day retreat | Access concentration; some hit 1st jhana on retreat |
| **Years 1–3** | Daily + 1–3 month retreats (Pa-Auk main center, Mawlamyine; or Pyin Oo Lwin; international branches exist) | Four jhanas + masteries |
| **Years 3–10, full-time** | Kasina curriculum, 8 attainments, 14 masteries | Part B complete — the launchpad |
| **Beyond** | Part C procedures under a teacher who claims the territory | Texts say: possible. Recorded history says: no verified case |
| **Parallel track, any level** | Part D insight on whatever concentration you have | #6 — the only one the Buddha called the point |

Non-negotiables from every source: sīla as the floor, one method (don't mix), a qualified teacher once you pass access concentration, and the SN 51.20 energy-tuning on every single sit.
