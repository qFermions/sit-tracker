# Northwind Robotics — Fleet Pro pricing update

June 12, 2026 — Northwind Robotics, Inc. today updated the published price
of its Fleet Pro fleet-management subscription from $49 per month to $79 per
month, effective July 1, 2026. The Basic tier remains $19 per month.

"Fleet Pro remains the fastest way to coordinate mixed robot fleets across
sites," the company said in the update posted to its pricing page.
