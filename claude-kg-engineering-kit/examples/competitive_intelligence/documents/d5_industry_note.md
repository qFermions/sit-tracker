# Industry note: fleet software, all-in on the cloud

NRI — as Northwind Robotics is known to long-time customers — has bet its
margins on an all-cloud strategy for fleet management.

Meanwhile Northwind Logistics, the Ohio-based trucking broker unrelated to
the robotics vendor, reported record freight volumes this quarter.

Analysts also flagged "Project Helios," an unannounced initiative that could
belong to either Northwind Robotics or Aurora Dynamics, according to
conflicting supplier reports.
