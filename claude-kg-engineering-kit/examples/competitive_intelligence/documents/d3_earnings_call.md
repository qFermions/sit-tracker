# Northwind Robotics Q2 2026 earnings call (excerpt)

CFO Dana Reyes: "Gross margin declined to 41 percent this quarter, down from
48 percent a year ago. Cloud infrastructure costs for our connected fleet
services continue to pressure margins, and we expect that pressure to
persist through the rest of the year."

Asked about the recent Fleet Pro price change, Reyes called it "a direct
response to those infrastructure costs."
